# Visual Style & Scene Reference

## Art Style (Apply to all generated sprites & UI)
- Medium: 2D hand-drawn storybook illustration, fine uneven black ink outlines, textured watercolor and gouache washes on off-white cold-press paper.
- Shading: Flat cel-shaded watercolor washes with visible pigment edges. No smooth digital gradients, 3D rendering, airbrushing, or glossy vector lines.
- Palette: Warm cedar brown (#8C583A), pastel aqua (#BCE3E8), sage green (#88C478), soft pink (#F497A9), bright coral red (#E84A4A), soft candle yellow (#FFF0A5), warm paper background (#F5F3EE).

## Background Spatial Layout (16:9 Canvas)
- Tub & Characters (X: 12%–65%, Y: 45%–95%): Round brown wooden hot tub with three cats (gray cat on left, orange cat in center, white cat on right) and steps on the right.
- Active Gameplay / Steam Zone (X: 22%–60%, Y: 25%–52%): Open off-white wall with light blue steam wisps rising from the water. Use this zone to spawn and float interactive letters.
- Title Area (X: 28%–65%, Y: 8%–22%): Hand-drawn "Hot Tub ♡" sign in alternating red and pink letters.
- Right Prop Column (X: 68%–98%, Y: 0%–95%): Hanging plant, pink heart clock, shelf, towel rack, and baskets. Keep free of UI overlays.
